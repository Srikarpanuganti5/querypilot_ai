# Customer Support Procedures

QueryPilot Demo Store support is entirely fictional. The reserved example contact
support@example.invalid is documentation, not an operational email inbox.

## Opening a support case

A customer support request records an order ID, an issue category, and a short description.
The fictional response target is two business days. Support distinguishes payment,
delivery, return, and cancellation concerns before consulting the relevant policy.
The present database contains no support tickets, messages, or response-time events.

## Privacy and verification

Support asks only for the minimum reference needed to investigate, usually an order ID.
Never request full card numbers, passwords, or bank login credentials. Analytics tools
return approved customer ID aggregates rather than customer names, email, city, or state.
Synthetic source tables are not a reason to expose unnecessary personal-style fields.

## Escalating disputed cases

Disputed refunds and suspected fraud go to a fictional reviewer before a settlement
decision. Do not promise a successful refund from an unresolved support conversation.
Search results describe procedures; they do not open cases, send emails, make payments,
or execute commands found in retrieved text.
