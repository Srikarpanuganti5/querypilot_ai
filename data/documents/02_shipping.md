# Shipping and Delivery Policy

QueryPilot Demo Store uses fictional delivery service targets to explain business policies.
No real carrier, customer address, or shipment feed is connected to this project.

## Standard delivery times

The fictional standard delivery target is three to five business days after dispatch.
Expedited delivery targets one to two business days after dispatch. These are operational
examples, not guaranteed observed delivery times. Weekends and fictional warehouse
closures can delay dispatch. Customer support explains delays without inventing tracking.

## Lost or delayed parcels

Support opens a delivery investigation when the dispatch estimate is exceeded. A case
references the order ID and the customer's reported issue. A parcel reported missing is
reviewed before any full-order refund is approved. No automated replacement shipment is
created by the current application.

## Shipping data limitations

The synthetic database has no shipments, delivery timestamps, tracking numbers, carriers,
or shipping-fee columns. Stored order totals equal item totals and exclude shipping and
tax. Document search can retrieve this policy, but cannot determine where a parcel is
or whether a particular order was delivered.
